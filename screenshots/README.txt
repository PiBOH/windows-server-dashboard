Screenshots of the real interface, taken from docs\Dashboard-Preview.html
(sample data, English UI, browser window 1600x900):

    hero-dark.png        top of the page, dark theme    -> used in README.md
    dashboard-dark.png   the whole page, dark theme (full page, 1600x4287)
    dashboard-light.png  light theme
    settings.png         the settings panel
    languages.png        the language picker, 38 entries

The four 1600x900 files are the viewport, not the whole page: they are what
you see when the page opens. Every one of them starts from the top of the
page - a shot taken after a panel was closed can be scrolled, and a scrolled
shot is a different picture.

To refresh them, open the preview in a browser, pick English in the language
picker first (the choice is remembered by that browser, the shot must not be
in another language), then take each of the five shots and save it over the
file of the same name.
